Klippet Support

Answers to common questions, and a way to reach a human.

How do I save what I just copied?

Open Klippet and tap the Paste button at the bottom — the current clipboard becomes a snippet. Or run the Save Clipboard shortcut from the Shortcuts app, the Action Button, a widget, or Siri, and stay in the app you are in.

How do I get a snippet back?

Three ways: tap a snippet in the app to copy it; switch to the Klippet keyboard in any text field and tap the snippet to type it right there; or run the Retrieve Snippet shortcut and pick from the list — it puts the snippet back on the clipboard.

How do I enable the Klippet keyboard?

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Klippet. Then open the keyboard's settings and switch on Allow Full Access — that is what lets the keyboard reach the snippets stored on your device. In any app, hold the globe key and choose Klippet.

Why does the keyboard ask for Full Access?

iOS requires that switch for a keyboard to read the shared on-device container where your snippets live — in Klippet's case that is its sole purpose. The keyboard works entirely on this device; see the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Where are my snippets stored?

On your device, in storage shared between the app, the keyboard and the Shortcuts actions. The app works offline and needs no account.

How do I delete snippets?

Swipe left on a snippet to delete it, or remove everything at once in Settings → Clear All Snippets. Deleting the app removes all data with it.
