Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

This policy describes how Klippet handles your information. The app works with the minimum data necessary to function, and stores it on your device.

Data the App Uses

Snippets — the pieces of text you choose to save, either from the clipboard or by typing them in. They are stored on your device, in a container shared between the Klippet app, the Klippet keyboard and the Klippet actions in Shortcuts, so all three can show you the same list.

Clipboard Access

The app reads the clipboard only when you ask it to — by tapping the Paste button or by running the Save Clipboard shortcut. iOS shows its standard paste confirmation where it applies, and what you paste becomes a snippet on your device.

Keyboard and Full Access

The Klippet keyboard types snippets from the on-device shared container. iOS requires the Allow Full Access switch for a keyboard to reach that container — in Klippet's case that is the sole purpose of the switch. The keyboard works entirely on this device: everything it types comes from the snippets you saved, and your typing in other apps stays between you and the app you are typing in.

Storage and Sharing

As of the date of this policy: your snippets are stored locally on your device, and the app needs no account. A snippet leaves the app only through an action you take yourself — copying it to the clipboard, typing it with the keyboard, or sharing it with the share sheet. Hidden snippets are masked on screen only — they are stored on your device in the same way as every other snippet.

Links and the Network

When you save a link, the app asks that website for its title and preview image, so the snippet is recognisable in your list. That request goes to the site itself, the way opening it in a browser would; the site sees that a device asked for that page. Nothing is sent to the developer, and a hidden snippet is never looked up. Everything else in the app works without a network connection.

Camera

If you scan a document, the camera image is read on your device to recognise its text, and both stay on your device. The camera is used only while you have the scanner open.

Your Controls

View all your snippets in the app; delete any snippet with a swipe; clear everything at once (Settings → Clear All Snippets); switch the keyboard off in iOS Settings; uninstall the app to remove all data.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app and on the website with an updated date.
